Know your energy.

Three starter reads for the brand prototype.
FEATURE / 6 MIN READ

What are caffeine pouches, really?

Caffeine pouches are a portable concept format designed around convenience and control. Compared with coffee or canned drinks, the main differentiator is portability and a simpler ritual.

FORMULA / 5 MIN READ

Caffeine + L-Theanine

Many people like this pairing because it frames caffeine around a smoother, more composed focus profile. Final claims depend on finished formulation and market rules.

GUIDE / 4 MIN READ

Choosing your strength

A three-tier system makes the line easier to understand. Lower tiers are friendlier to lighter users, while the highest tier should be positioned carefully as a high-caffeine option.
