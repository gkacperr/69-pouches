FAQ

Simple answers.

What are caffeine pouches?

A pouch-format caffeine product concept designed to be used without a drink. Final use instructions and classification will depend on the finished product.

Which strength should I choose?

The current concept uses 75 mg, 150 mg and 250 mg tiers. The highest tier is high caffeine and should receive dedicated formulation and safety review before launch.

What's inside 69?

The current prototype is built around caffeine with concept amounts of L-Theanine, Alpha-GPC and Vitamin D3. Supporting ingredient amounts are not final.

How many pouches are in a can?

That is not locked yet. The website is structured so pouch count, net weight and formula details can be updated later.

When does 69 launch?

This is still a concept storefront. Final formulation, production, compliance and launch timing have not been confirmed.
