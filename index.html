What are caffeine pouches?

A pouch-format caffeine concept designed to support a portable, no-liquid routine. Final usage and classification depend on the finished product.

Which strength should I choose?

The concept uses 75 mg, 150 mg and 250 mg tiers. The highest tier is a high-caffeine option and should be positioned responsibly.

The current prototype centers on caffeine with concept support from L-Theanine, Alpha-GPC and Vitamin D3.
