What are caffeine pouches?

They are a pouch-format caffeine product concept designed to be used without a drink. The final product format, serving instructions and regulatory classification still need to be confirmed.

Are the 75 / 150 / 250 mg values final?

No. The three tiers are part of the current store concept. The 250 mg tier is intentionally marked as high caffeine and should receive dedicated safety and formulation review before commercialization.

How many pouches will be in one can?

Not decided yet. The website is structured so that pouch count, net weight, strength and pricing can be updated later from one data source.

Why include L-Theanine?

It is currently included as a supporting concept ingredient to sit alongside caffeine. Exact dose and any final claims must be based on the finished formulation and evidence.

What is Alpha-GPC doing in the formula?

Alpha-GPC is shown as a choline-source component in the performance positioning. Its final inclusion and dose are not yet locked.

Can I combine 69 with coffee or energy drinks?

The concept should not encourage excessive caffeine stacking. Final packaging should include clear serving guidance and warnings based on the finished caffeine dose.

Is checkout live?

No. The current cart is a front-end demo only. Stripe, Shopify or another checkout can be connected later.

Will every flavor launch at once?

That is the current visual plan, but the final launch lineup can be reduced or phased without redesigning the store.
