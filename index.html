FAQ

Questions
before launch.

Are these the final formulas?

No. Every non-caffeine ingredient amount on this prototype is a placeholder. The purpose of this version is visual direction, UX and product architecture.

Are all seven flavors available in every strength?

That is how this mock store is structured. You can later restrict certain flavors to specific tiers without changing the overall design.

Does the cart take real payments?

Not yet. The cart is a functioning front-end demo. Stripe, Shopify or another checkout can be connected after the product and legal setup are finalized.

Why is 250 mg marked MAX?

It is a high-caffeine concept tier, so the interface deliberately separates it visually. Final dose, serving instructions and warnings should be reviewed before commercialization.
